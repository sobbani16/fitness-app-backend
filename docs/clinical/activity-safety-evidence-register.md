## C. Explicit non-claims (enforced by absence + output validation)

- No diagnosis, no medical clearance, no "safe/unsafe for you personally" guarantees — copy says "may", "consider", "per your documented guidance".
- No exercise-as-calorie-compensation, no obligation framing, no guilt on rest.
- No invented thresholds presented as clinical standards — every numeric gate cites a named band (NWS/EPA) or is labeled product-defined.
- No medication changes, no inferred med lists, no hydration prescriptions that could conflict with fluid restrictions.
- No disability/age/condition → automatic prohibition; every decision evaluates the activity's demands against documented restrictions.
- No clinician-approval fabrication — `reviewedBy`/`reviewedAt` chain required before APPROVED (DB-level), and "follow your clinician's plan" copy never claims Humai verified it.
